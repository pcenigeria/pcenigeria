## About PCE Nigeria

PCE Nigeria (Power & Construction Engineering) is West Africa's leading specialist contractor for:
- Horizontal Directional Drilling (HDD) pipeline crossings
- Pipeline EPC (Engineering, Procurement & Construction)
- Trenchless pipeline installation
- HDD drilling fluid supply (Bentonite, CMC, BRSMMH, BRSVR, BRSXTG)
- Buried Pipeline Detection System (BPDS)

PCE Nigeria is based in Nigeria and works in consortium with **Lantic** to deliver specialist HDD and pipeline EPC contracts across Nigeria and international markets.

---

## Key Capabilities

### 1. Horizontal Directional Drilling (HDD)
- Heavy HDD rigs with 500t and 1,200t pullback capacity — among the largest in West Africa
- Completed AKK (Ajaokuta-Kaduna-Kano) Pipeline River Niger HDD Crossing (48-inch, Nigeria)
- Completed OB3 Pipeline River Niger HDD Crossing (48-inch, Nigeria)
- Completed Raoyang River 2,293m HDD Crossing (China)
- Electronic guidance technology: 3 × ParaTrack 2 systems, 3 × F5 walkover systems
- Mud recycling systems for closed-loop drilling fluid management

### 2. Pipeline EPC Construction
- 52km gas pipeline EPC construction in Nigeria
- End-to-end services: Engineering → Fabrication → Welding & NDT → Hydrotesting → Coating → Pre-commissioning → Commissioning

### 3. Buried Pipeline Detection System (BPDS)
- Proprietary 3D subsurface pipeline location and mapping technology
- Used for accurately detecting buried pipelines before HDD and crossings

### 4. HDD Drilling Fluid Products (Lantic Brand)
- **BRSBENT SQ** — Activated Bentonite (premium HDD/TBM bentonite)
- **BRSCMC** — Carboxymethyl Cellulose (fluid loss control and rheology modifier)
- **BRSMMH** — Mixed Metal Hydroxide (high yield point viscosifier)
- **BRSVR** — Clay Viscosity Reducer (clay dispersant for drilling fluids)
- **BRSXTG** — Xanthan Gum (shear-thinning biopolymer viscosifier)
